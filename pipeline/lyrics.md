# TECH(NO) US — choir lyrics

Short sung phrases for the melodic choir passes (choir.py). No tickers, no
company names. Each line is one phrase; vocals.py / descensus.py pick lines
by index.

2. Then the machines began to dream
3. Higher and higher, four sigma higher
4. One quiet morning the east woke up
5. Half of tomorrow was gone by noon
6. And still the curve climbs on
